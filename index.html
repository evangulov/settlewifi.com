Questions

A speed test shows megabits. Settle turns the measurement into an answer: is the network good enough for websites, calls and big files. The numbers stay in the details.

macOS only shares Wi‑Fi network names together with Location access. Settle uses the names to recognise familiar networks. It doesn’t use your location, and the check works without this access too.

Settle notices the sign-in page and offers to open it. The check starts once you’re in.

No. Settle rates the network at the moment of the check. If response times rise noticeably later, you get a notification — when notifications are on.

To measure, Settle contacts test servers run by Apple and Cloudflare. Your check history stays on your Mac. No account, no product analytics.
