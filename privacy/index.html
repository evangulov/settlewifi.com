Privacy

What happens to data on settlewifi.com and in the Settle app for Mac. They work differently, so each is described separately.

Updated: 3 October 2026

  • Website visit statistics are kept by Google Analytics, and only if you allow it — it then sets its own cookies.
  • The app has no account, analytics or crash reports.
  • To check a network, the app contacts test servers run by Cloudflare, Selectel and Apple. Network names and history are not sent there.
  • Check history and logs stay on your Mac.

On this page

WebsiteAppQuestions

settlewifi.com

No ads or social pixels. The only outside script is Google Analytics statistics: it loads and sets its cookies only after you agree. The website itself sets no cookies. Fonts are your system’s.

Visit statistics

Why
To understand how many people visit, where from, and what they do next: whether they download Settle, go to support the project or to LinkedIn.
When
Only after you agree: “Accept all”, or “Analytics” switched on in the settings. Until then the statistics script isn’t loaded, and events aren’t collected to be sent later.
Events
Page visit; click on “Download for Mac” (a click, not an installation); going to support the project; going to LinkedIn.
What data
Page address, referrer, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, country and region derived from the IP address, the number in the _ga cookies, and the events below. According to Google, Google Analytics 4 doesn’t log or store IP addresses.
Who handles it
Google (Google Ireland Limited; data may be transferred to Google LLC, USA). Ads, Google signals and granular location are off in the Settle property.
How long it’s kept
Visit- and user-level data — 14 months; Google keeps aggregated reports longer.
Not collected
Session recordings, ad tracking, data from the Settle app — network names, measurements.
How to turn off
Click “Privacy settings” at the bottom of the page and switch off “Analytics”. Events stop being sent, and statistics entries the site can reach in your browser are removed.

What the website keeps in your browser

settle.lang
Your language choice — appears when you choose “Русский” or “English” in the header, menu or footer. It keeps the English page from switching you to the Russian one. Until you pick a language yourself, the English page looks at your browser’s main language and opens the Russian version if it’s Russian — on your device, not stored anywhere.
settle.consent
Your statistics choice, so the site doesn’t ask on every page.
_ga
A random browser number for Google Analytics, so it can tell a new visit from a returning one. Lasts 13 months.
_ga_L03GHL6DVM
The current visit’s number and time for Google Analytics. Lasts 13 months.

settle.lang and settle.consent are entries in the browser’s local storage, not cookies, and are never sent to the server. The _ga cookies are set by Google Analytics, and only after you agree. If you withdraw consent in “Privacy settings”, the site removes them.

No language choice is saved right now.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it receives technical request data: IP address, page address, browser details and time. Whether and how long it keeps them is up to its own rules.

The Settle app for Mac

The app has no account, analytics, crash reports or telemetry.

What the app connects to

captive.apple.com
Every check

To see whether there’s internet and whether the network wants you to sign in. The request goes over plain HTTP, same as macOS’s own check.

www.apple.com, www.cloudflare.com
Every check

To check access and measure response time.

speed.cloudflare.com
Speed checks

To download a test file and upload test data — zero bytes. When your phone shares its internet — up to 5 MB per check.

speedtest.selectel.ru
If Cloudflare is unavailable

The same speed check through Selectel’s servers in Russia: a test file and zero bytes. When your phone shares its internet — up to 5 MB.

Apple servers via networkQuality
If neither Cloudflare nor Selectel answers

Settle runs networkQuality, a tool built into macOS, which connects to Apple’s servers. Never when your phone shares its internet: this check uses a lot of data.

settlewifi.com
Once a day

To check for updates: settlewifi.com/appcast.xml. If there is one, the archive downloads from settlewifi.com/download/, and its signature is verified before installing.

What these requests carry

Address
Only the check’s service address. The download address includes the test file size.
Body
Only the upload check has one — zero bytes. Network names, history and other data from your Mac are not sent.
Headers
What macOS adds to any app request: Settle’s name and build number, CFNetwork and Darwin versions (they reveal the macOS version), preferred languages, supported compression. The update request also names the Sparkle version; Settle doesn’t send system profile data.
What the server sees
As with any connection — IP address and time. Whether and how long Cloudflare, Selectel, Apple and the hosting keep request logs is up to their own rules.

Check requests don’t keep cookies or cache.

What stays on your Mac

Check history
The last 10 checks for each network: network name, time, result and measurements. Also access point addresses (BSSID), so Settle can tell “Guest” in one café from “Guest” in another. Kept in the app’s preferences.
Settings
Whether to show text in the menu bar, whether the first run is done, whether you answered the notifications offer.
Log
~/Library/Logs/Settle.log — measurement times, results and errors, without network names or page addresses. Once it passes 1 MB, it’s trimmed to the last 256 KB.
Apple check results
~/Library/Logs/Settle/checks — raw networkQuality output, the last 20.

How to delete

History only: Settle settings → “Clear history”.

Removing everything erases the check history and all Settle settings. This can’t be undone. Quit Settle first (settings → “Quit Settle”), then run in Terminal:

defaults delete com.settlewifi.Settle
rm -rf ~/Library/Logs/Settle ~/Library/Logs/Settle.log

Deleting the app itself doesn’t erase this data.

Permissions

Location
macOS shares Wi‑Fi network names only with apps that have Location access. Settle asks for it to recognise familiar networks and doesn’t request coordinates. Without it the check still works, but familiar networks aren’t recognised. To change: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
Notifications
Optional. Used to tell you the result of a check and when the connection gets worse. Notifications are created on your Mac, without servers.
Open at login
Optional, a switch in Settle settings.
